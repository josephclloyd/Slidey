## What worked
- Parallelising all 5 issues at once — only SlideshowView.swift was a hot file, and the #107 and #95 branches were serialised implicitly by the order CI ran
- The `coreView` split pattern: extracting `emptyStateContent`, `imageDisplayContent`, `overlayViews` @ViewBuilder properties then isolating the `body`-level modifier chain into a `coreView` computed property reliably resolves SwiftUI type-checker timeouts in Xcode 16.3 CI. Applied on both #95 and #107.
- The merge commit approach for conflict resolution: running `git merge origin/main` on the #107 branch after #95 merged let git identify the three conflict regions precisely, making manual resolution straightforward.

## What didn't
- Predicted no merge conflicts in the sprint plan ("all 5 issues touch distinct files") — wrong. Both #95 and #107 independently extracted @ViewBuilder properties from SlideshowView.swift, producing a three-region conflict after #95 merged first.
- SlideshowView type-checker timeout was not predicted despite having been hit in Sprint 11. CLAUDE.md should warn about it.
- Two repair rounds were needed on both #107 and #95: first pass extracted @ViewBuilder properties but left the body modifier chain too long; second pass added the `coreView` split to fix CI.
- ImageLoaderTests.swift merge conflict on detect-directory-gone: the branch's own tests (DirectoryMissingTests) conflicted with ImageLoaderRenameTests added by #96 which merged into main first.

## Patterns established
- **SlideshowView hot file**: any branch that adds to overlayViews, coreView, or body will conflict with a sibling branch doing the same. The sprint planner's "distinct files" check must also inspect *which properties* within SlideshowView are being added to.
- **@ViewBuilder extraction + coreView split** is now the established fix for SlideshowView type-checker timeouts. It should be documented as a gotcha in CLAUDE.md.
- **Merge into feature branch before done phase** when two branches both touch SlideshowView — do `git merge origin/main` on the later branch before attempting merge, don't just wait for GitHub to report it mergeable.
