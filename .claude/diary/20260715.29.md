## What didn't
- **Extra-usage budget kept climbing sprint over sprint** (85.2% → 89.1% at the start of
  consecutive sprints) with no sign of leveling off. Flagged to Joe twice this sprint
  alone (once at the monthly level, once at the five-hour level right before the biggest
  issue). Worth watching whether the pace of back-to-back sprints needs to slow down
  before the monthly ceiling actually gets hit mid-issue.
- **A ~3-hour quota wait outlasted the persistent monitor's 1-hour timeout**, and this
  wasn't caught until manually checking status well after the wait began — the monitor
  had silently stopped delivering events. Worth building in a habit of restarting the
  monitor proactively before or during any wait expected to exceed an hour, not just
  reactively after noticing a gap.

## Patterns established
- **Long quota waits (>1h) need the monitor restarted, not just scheduled wakeups.** A
  `ScheduleWakeup` alone gets the orchestrator re-invoked at the right time, but the
  Monitor task backing the event stream has its own independent timeout and will not
  survive a wait longer than it. Check `mcx status`/restart the monitor explicitly after
  any wait that could plausibly exceed an hour.
- **Triage live bug reports (read the actual code path) before filing the issue**, not
  just after — validated a second time this sprint (following #219's post-merge fixes'
  pattern). A root-caused issue is materially more actionable for a future sprint than a
  symptom description alone.
